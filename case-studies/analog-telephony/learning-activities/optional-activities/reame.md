# Optional Learning Activities

This directory contains complementary learning activities used within Module 20 — Connection to the Telephone Network.

These activities are part of the broader instructional module but are documented separately from the core Analog Telephony Laboratory sequence because they develop additional telecommunications competencies rather than representing required stages of the primary service path.

The core sequence documented in the parent directory focuses on:

**Customer Premises → Distribution → Outside Plant → Functional Service**

The activities presented here extend that environment into additional telecommunications systems and applications.

---

## Coaxial Cable Distribution

**Document:** [coaxial-cable-distribution.pdf](coaxial-cable-distribution.pdf)

This activity introduces students to coaxial cable installation and distribution.

Students perform practical tasks including:

- installation of coaxial cable between the student workspace and the distribution splitter;
- termination of the cable with a male coaxial connector;
- installation of a faceplate with a female coaxial connector;
- testing of the completed installation.

The activity complements the twisted-pair and structured-cabling activities by exposing students to another physical medium used in telecommunications distribution.

The laboratory environment can also support functional signal testing, allowing students to verify the completed coaxial installation rather than evaluating the termination only visually.

---

## Analog PABX Configuration

**Document:** [analog-pabx-configuration.pdf](analog-pabx-configuration.pdf)

This activity extends the use of the analog telephone infrastructure into a small private telephone system.

Students connect previously installed telephone lines to an analog PABX and perform configuration and operational tasks involving:

- C.O. line connection;
- extension configuration;
- incoming and outgoing call routing;
- ringing configuration;
- recorded messages;
- music on hold;
- call transfer;
- conference calling.

The activity demonstrates how the physical telephone infrastructure developed during earlier laboratory work can support higher-level telecommunications services.

Rather than treating the PABX as an isolated device, the activity connects it to telephone lines already installed within the student work areas.

---

## Relationship to the Core Case Study

These activities are intentionally classified as optional or complementary within this repository.

They demonstrate that the same vocational laboratory environment can support multiple telecommunications learning scenarios without requiring each activity to be part of a single linear sequence.

This modular approach allows instructors to select activities according to curriculum requirements, available equipment, instructional time, and learning objectives.

The activities also illustrate a broader principle of the project: existing laboratory infrastructure can be reused and reconfigured to support different authentic telecommunications tasks.
